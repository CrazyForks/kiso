# DC-56 — an ask panel locks the terminal's scrollback: the history cannot be read while a question is up

- **id:** DC-56
- **class:** TUI / mouse mode vs the terminal's scrollback
- **severity:** **P2.** Not a crash and not data loss — the transcript is
  still on screen and in the log — but the one surface where a person
  reads back what just streamed is disabled for as long as a question is
  open, and nothing says so. With a long streamed answer and a panel at
  the bottom, the text you need is a wheel-turn away and unreachable.
- **agent:** kiso 0.40.0 (TUI2-R3v2 ②'s mouse policy), reported from a
  real session.
- **found by:** the owner — "while an ask is up, the streamed content
  above cannot be scrolled" (their report, rendered in English).
- **status:** **FIXED** in this round, red before green; the ruling is
  recorded here (the owner's ruling of 2026-09-21).

## The mechanism (all three facts are in the code)

1. Mouse reporting is turned ON when a selection surface opens —
   `packages/tui/src/editor.ts`, `#syncMouse()`:
   `panelInput.up() || pickInput.up() || atUp()`, with `MOUSE_ON =
   "\x1b[?1000h\x1b[?1006h"` written to the terminal.
2. **While a terminal is reporting mouse events it does not scroll its own
   scrollback** — the wheel arrives as an SGR report instead
   (`\x1b[<64;col;rowM`), for the application to interpret.
3. kiso drops wheel reports by design: `#mouseEvent` starts with
   `if (button !== 0) return;` — "scrolling past a panel must not answer
   it" — and `packages/tui/tests/tui2-r3v2-mouse.test.ts` asserts exactly
   that for buttons 64/65.

So during a panel: the terminal will not scroll (1+2) and kiso does not
scroll (3). The transcript above the live region is unreachable until the
question is answered. Outside a panel everything works, which is why the
defect only ever shows up at the worst moment — after a long stream, when
the model stops to ask.

The gesture that bought this was narrower than it looked: **only the
panel consumed a click** (`bindPanelRows` → `panelOptionRows`); the
session picker and the @ picker enabled reporting and handled nothing.

## The ruling (the owner's, 2026-09-21)

*The history wins.* kiso never enables reporting; every surface keeps its
keys; the resets (entry/exit/editor handover) and the SGR parse path stay,
because a terminal left reporting by something else must still never leak
a byte into the composer — and in that foreign case the panel's click
still answers it.

## Red before green

- `packages/tui/tests/tui2-r3v2-mouse.test.ts` — **red 2 / green 11**: the
  panel emitted the enable, and the close emitted a disable for a mode
  that had never been on.
- `apps/cli/tests/tui2-r3v2-mouse-pty.test.ts` (real pty, byte-asserted) —
  **red 1 / green 7**: on the pre-patch build a panel's enable is in the
  raw stream; now no surface emits one, while the reset invariant and the
  injected-click path both still hold.

## What is still true afterwards (and is NOT this defect)

- A terminal left in mouse mode by a killed process still reports clicks
  until the next kiso resets it — that is the pre-existing defensive path,
  unchanged.
- Scroll while a *picker* is up is now the terminal's own again as well;
  the picker's filter and arrows are unchanged.
