## 0430-B1 — adjudicated: the raw-text replay is withdrawn before the release

evidence on the finding, not a second gate): median cost +12.4%, wall
+13.4%, the rc cheaper in 2 of 6, the expensive shape twice more on the
rc side and never on the control's. Pooled 18 pairs: median +3.3% /
+3.9% (PASS by the margins), rc requests 591 vs control 557, the
